A one-line joke about a smart fridge that takes a sleep-talked idiom literally. At 3 a.m. it hears "I'm in a pickle", spends $87 on pickles, and leaves its owner with 214 jars — plus a delivery driver who has taken out a restraining order. The laugh comes from the escalation: a harmless turn of phrase turns into an expensive, absurd, legally awkward mountain of pickles, and the final twist lands on a person, not a punchline about the fridge. Running `python3 main.py` prints the joke and also writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
